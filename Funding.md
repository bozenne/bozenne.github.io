# GREEN-CARE

I am the happy beneficiary of a Horizon Europe grant (2027-2030) on
the topic 'towards climate resilient, prepared and carbon neutral
populations and healthcare systems'. The project, led by Max Piffoux,
is entitled 'Greening Healthcare Decision-Making and care pathways
through Environmental Impact Integration and its Application in
Oncology'. 

Here are the first sentences of the abstract of the project: "The
project will design, prototype and validate an
interoperabledecision-support toolbox integrating multi-impact life
cycle assessment (LCA) into healthcare innovation and
decision-making. It will be applied as a proof-of-concept in oncology,
a high-stakes and data-rich specialty. The objectives are to:
- (i) quantify environmental footprints beyond carbon across care pathways (e.g. drugs release, microparticles, etc)
- (ii) translate these results into decision-ready indicators with transparent uncertainty
- (iii) embed these indicators into operational workflows for procurement, clinical trials, health technology assessment (HTA), and care pathway optimisation.

![](https://bozenne.github.io/img/GREENCARE-WP.png) *Overview of the work packages and their relations.*

I will be responsible for work package 6, described below.

## Work package 6: Integrating environmental footprint in clinical trial endpoints

*Objectives*: Develop, validate and disseminate methods for
integrating Environmental footprint and impact in clinical trial
statistical design and analyses through composite endpoints and
multicriteria analyses.

The work package is divided into 4 tasks and I will mainly be active in the first two:
- T6.1: Development of an estimator of time-to-event endpoints
adjusted for environmental footprint. Convert pollutant emissions
generated along patient care pathways into future health losses (DALYs
or life-years lost (YLL)) to create an estimator of time to event
endpoints adjusted for environmental footprint.
- T6.2: Integration of environmental emissions in multicriteria
analyses using generalized pairwise comparisons. Will extend the
current GPC framework to analyze environmental impact alongside
clinical endpoints.
- T6.3: Application to illustrative examples and dissemination within
  the medical community
- T6.4: Development of guidance for investigators on how to integrate
  environmental emissions in trial designs according to context
